Questions, bugs, or ideas: [bodkin.support@gmail.com](mailto:bodkin.support@gmail.com). For a bug, it helps to mention your device, your iOS version and what you were doing.

## Projects and backup

**Where are my projects?** On your iPhone or iPad. Bodkin has no account and no server, so nothing is stored online. Open the Files app → On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Bodkin to see them: each canvas or chart is a `.bodkin` file you can copy somewhere safe, rename or delete. Chart files you import are the designer's work, so they stay inside Bodkin instead.

**How do I back them up?** Copy the `.bodkin` files from the Files app. Your projects are also part of your device's iCloud or computer backup, like other app data.

**Send a canvas to someone:** on the home screen, touch and hold the canvas, then tap Share. They can open the `.bodkin` file in Bodkin on their iPhone or iPad.

**Moving to a new iPhone or iPad?** Restore the new device from a backup of the old one and your projects come with it. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases (below).

## Purchases

**What's free?** The app, every painted canvas, the basic stitches and every tool. Your first five projects made from photos, chart pictures or blank canvas or Aida are free too, and so is your first imported chart file.

**What do the purchases add?** Both are one-time purchases, with no subscription:
- **Bodkin Pro:** unlimited projects from photos, chart pictures and blank canvas or Aida, as many imported chart files as you like, Design mode on any design, names and dates lettered onto any design, a choice of stitched app icons, and every Stitch Pack stitch.
- **Stitch Pack:** decorative and specialty stitches for both crafts: Bargello, Brick, Mosaic, Scotch, Smyrna, Rice, Slanted Gobelin, Milanese, Cashmere and Hungarian for needlepoint, and Double Cross, Algerian Eye, Rhodes, Long-Arm Cross and Upright Cross for both. Beads and French knots are free.

**Restore a purchase:** tap the yellow Bodkin Pro button (a crown) at the top right of the home screen, or any locked stitch under More Stitches (the four squares at the bottom of the stitch rail), then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Family Sharing:** both purchases work with Family Sharing.

## Stitching

**Needlepoint, cross-stitch or both?** Bodkin asks when you first open it. To change it later, tap the gear on the home screen → I Stitch. Choose Both and each new project asks which craft it is.

**Can I import a chart?** Yes: on the home screen tap Import a Chart and pick an `.oxs` file, the open format chart design programs export. You can also open an `.oxs` file from Files and choose Bodkin.

**Tracking a project you're stitching for real:** open a chart or canvas, tap the sliders button → Mode → Track My Stitching, and tap squares to mark them done. Progress & Pace, in the ⋯ menu beside it, shows your pace and a projected finish date.

**Do I need an Apple Pencil?** No. You can stitch with a fingertip on iPhone and iPad. On iPad, choose My Finger on the welcome screen. Apple Pencil takes over as soon as it touches the canvas, and fingers pan and zoom.

**Where are a canvas's settings?** Open a canvas and tap the sliders button at the top. Hand-Stitched mode (also the hand button on the tool rail) and Even Tension are there. Lettering, Stitch It for Real and Replay Tips are in the ⋯ menu beside it, and Sounds and Haptics in the home screen's gear menu.

**Accessibility:** Bodkin works with VoiceOver (including stitching), Voice Control, Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and Smart Invert. If something gets in your way, please email us.

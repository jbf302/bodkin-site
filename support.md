Questions, bugs, or ideas: [bodkin.support@gmail.com](mailto:bodkin.support@gmail.com). For a bug, it helps to mention your device, your iOS version and what you were doing.

## Projects and backup

**Where are my projects?** On your iPhone or iPad. Bodkin has no account and no server, so nothing is stored online.

**How do I back them up?** Your projects are part of your device's iCloud or computer backup, like other app data.

**Moving to a new iPhone or iPad?** Restore the new device from a backup of the old one and your projects come with it. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases (below).

## Purchases

**What's free?** The app, every painted canvas, the basic stitches and every tool. Your first five canvases made from photos, charts or blank mesh are free too.

**What do the purchases add?** Both are one-time purchases, with no subscription:
- **Bodkin Pro:** unlimited canvases from photos, charts and blank mesh, names and dates lettered onto any painted canvas, and every Stitch Pack stitch.
- **Stitch Pack:** five decorative stitches: Bargello, Brick, Mosaic, Scotch and Smyrna.

**Restore a purchase:** tap the yellow sparkle button (Bodkin Pro) at the top right of the home screen, or any stitch with a lock, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Family Sharing:** both purchases work with Family Sharing.

## Stitching

**Do I need an Apple Pencil?** No. You can stitch with a fingertip on iPhone and iPad. On iPad, choose My Finger on the welcome screen. Apple Pencil takes over as soon as it touches the canvas, and fingers pan and zoom.

**Where are a canvas's settings?** Open a canvas and tap the sliders button at the top. Hand-Stitched mode, Even Tension, Sounds and Haptics are there.

**Accessibility:** Bodkin works with VoiceOver (including stitching), Voice Control, Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and Smart Invert. If something gets in your way, please email us.

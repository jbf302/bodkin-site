Bodkin is a needlepoint app for iPad. It has no accounts and doesn't collect, track, or sell your data.

## What stays on your device
- Your projects, stash, and settings are stored on your iPad only. If you turn on file sharing, they show up in the Files app so you can back them up.
- Photos or chart images you choose to convert are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

## What we collect
Nothing. The developer receives no analytics, crash reports, or personal information from the app.

## Third parties
- **Purchases:** the optional Stitch Pack is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.
- **Shopping links:** the shopping list opens Amazon in your browser. Amazon's own privacy policy applies once you leave the app.

## Children
Bodkin doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

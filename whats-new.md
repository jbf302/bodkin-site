## Version 1.3

### Cross-stitch, as a craft of its own
Bodkin now does cross-stitch as fully as needlepoint. Tell it what you stitch (needlepoint, cross-stitch or both) and every screen speaks your craft.

- Stitch on Aida with full, half, ¾ and ¼ stitches, French knots, beads and backstitch, in floss that looks like floss.
- Charts show symbols, numbers or color only, just like the printed chart.
- Turn any photo or painted design into a cross-stitch chart, with a DMC shopping list and a printable symbol chart.
- Cross-Stitch 101, a practice lesson on Aida, and a practice swatch for every stitch, fractionals too.

### Bring your own chart
Import an .oxs chart file from your design program, Files, Mail or AirDrop, with its half stitches, fractionals, French knots, beads and backstitch. Your first imported chart is free.

### Track a real project
Stitching a chart on real fabric or canvas? Mark squares done as you go. A row guide keeps your place, parked threads show where you left each color, and Progress & Pace shows your best day and when you'll finish.

### Design mode
Paint a canvas or chart your own design square by square: draw, erase, fill and pick colors with a choice of brush sizes. Swap one thread for another, or merge two, everywhere at once.

### More stitches
Beads, free for everyone. The Stitch Pack adds Rice, Slanted Gobelin, Milanese, Cashmere and Hungarian for needlepoint, and Double Cross, Algerian Eye, Rhodes, Long-Arm Cross and Upright Cross for both crafts.

### Looks more like the real thing
Wool, floss, metallics, canvas and Aida were redrawn to look like real stitching, with fuller French knots and cleaner stitch edges.

### And
- A new stitched app icon, and more icons to choose from with Bodkin Pro.
- A shorter tool rail: the everyday stitches stay on it, the decorative and specialty stitches open from More Stitches with their names, and the last one you used stays on the rail.
- Sounds, Haptics, Replay Tips and Help & Support are in the home screen's settings.
- New designs: Apple a Day, a chart rounded off with ¾ stitches, plus a Martini, Tinned Sardines, a Lemon, Cherries, a Fox and an Owl.
- Fixed a crash on Mac when making a canvas from a photo.

## Version 1.2

### Your canvases, in the Files app
Every canvas is now its own file in Files under Bodkin. Back them up, or share one with a friend: touch and hold a canvas on the home screen and tap Share.

### Bigger canvases
Up to 300 stitches each way for everyone, and up to 600 with Bodkin Pro. There's also a Large Pillow size.

### A better printed chart
Evenly split pages with their own color keys, a cover with sizes and a supplies checklist, a how-to-read page, Letter or A4, black-and-white symbols, and a progress tracker to color in.

### Wool
- All 430 Appleton wool shades and 283 Persian (Paternayan) shades in the Thread Box, in photo import, on the shopping list and in My Stash.
- Photos now convert to Appleton wool, and canvases up to 18 mesh start with Appleton on the shopping list. Pick another thread any time.
- Nine metallics, bought as Kreinik braid.

### Stitch It for Real, closer to hand
- A Stitch It for Real card on the home screen starts from a photo and ends with the shopping list and chart.
- Open the shopping list and chart from a canvas's settings or its touch-and-hold menu.
- The canvas image for a printer or painter is now the whole cut canvas, with margins, squaring marks and a thread key.
- Tidy up: fold stray stitches into their neighbors when converting a photo.
- Crop your photo in From a Photo, and touch and hold a color to merge it into another.
- On iPad, From a Photo shows your photo and its canvas side by side.
- Removing a photo's background? Pick the color it's stitched in.
- Any canvas mesh, from 5 to 40, for blank and photo canvases, shopping lists and prints, with 12, 14, 28 and 32 added as quick picks.

### Shop for it
- Send your whole list to your local needlepoint shop.
- Silk and overdyed swaps suggested for each color.
- The list now includes the small tools.

### Stitching feel
- Hand-Stitched is now on the tool rail.
- Parked needles: switch colors and your needle waits with the thread still on it.
- Touching a spot painted for another color lights up that thread, instead of showing a message.
- Squeeze or double-tap your Apple Pencil to open your threads at its tip.

### Gallery and streak
See every finished piece, framed, with your totals and your stitching streak.

### New canvases
Lobster, blue crab and whale tail, plus a new Just for Fun shelf of sports.

## Version 1.1

### Bodkin now works on iPhone Duo
Stitch on the big inner screen or the outer one, in any orientation. Half-fold it like a book and the controls sit on the left with your canvas on the right, clear of the fold, and your gallery spreads across both sides.

### Smoother stitching
- New Even Tension option: turn it on under Hand-Stitched and every stitch comes out just right, so your work looks even while you get the feel of the pull.
- Just-right tension is easier to hit when you hand stitch with a finger.
- Slimmer tent stitches with tapered ends that look more like wool.
- Mosaic, Scotch and Smyrna stitches now compensate at the edges of a shape, the way you would on real canvas.
- When you're zoomed in close, new stitches appear right away, and old ones no longer peek through after a fill, undo or pan.
- No more flickering shadows when you stitch over or next to another stitch.

### New canvases
- Winter holidays: wreath, snowflake and cardinal ornaments, a gingerbread man, nutcracker, dreidel, menorah, snowman and cozy mittens.
- Dogs: Yellow, Black and Chocolate Labs, a Golden Retriever, a Dachshund and a French Bulldog.
- Patterns: Houndstooth, Preppy Stripes and Hungarian Point.

### Easier for everyone
- VoiceOver can now stitch the canvas, fill in every spot of a color, read each skein's progress and tell you when a color is finished.
- Clearer text that also responds to Increase Contrast, bigger tap targets, and more icons that grow with your text size.
- Reduce Motion, Smart Invert and Voice Control are supported throughout.
- Haptics have their own switch in canvas settings, separate from Sounds.
- Undo and redo with Command-Z and Shift-Command-Z on a keyboard.
